## Observation
Objective criterion O5 ("Dependencies/blockers named or explicitly none — silence is a miss") is ambiguous under the file tracker. Its wording assumes a prose declaration in the story body, but the file backend has a structured, machine-owned `dependsOn:` front-matter field that is empty by default. An empty `dependsOn` field with no prose statement is arguably an explicit "none," yet a literal reading of "silence is a miss" would fail it. I passed O5 by treating the present-but-empty structured field (corroborated by the dependency gate returning NONE) as an explicit declaration, but the criterion does not say whether the structured field counts. This will recur on every file-tracker story that has no dependencies and doesn't restate "no dependencies" in prose.
